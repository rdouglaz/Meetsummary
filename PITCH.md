# MeetSummary — Pitch & Explainer Script
### Positioning: Virtual Assistant → Client

---

## ONE-LINER

> **MeetSummary turns every client meeting into a structured, searchable, action-ready record — automatically.**

---

## THE PROBLEM (30 seconds)

Every Virtual Assistant knows this moment:

A client call ends. You have scattered notes, half-remembered decisions, and a follow-up email to write — all while the next task is already waiting. Manually transcribing, summarising, and chasing action items eats hours every week. And when something slips through the cracks, it's your reputation on the line.

---

## THE SOLUTION (60 seconds)

MeetSummary is a meeting intelligence platform built for VAs who manage client relationships at scale.

You upload or record a meeting — from Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp, phone, or any audio source — and within minutes MeetSummary delivers:

- **A full, speaker-labelled transcript** — searchable word by word
- **An AI-generated executive summary** — key points, decisions, and risks
- **Action items, automatically extracted** — with owners and due dates
- **A ready-to-send follow-up email** — drafted by AI, edited by you, sent in one click
- **CRM sync** — pushed straight to HubSpot or Salesforce, no copy-paste
- **Slack or Teams notification** — your client's team is informed instantly

Everything is stored securely, tagged, and retrievable. Nothing falls through the cracks.

## KEY DIFFERENTIATORS

1. **End-to-end workflow** — from audio to CRM, nothing leaves the platform mid-process
2. **Speaker identification** — label once, applied everywhere (transcript, summary, action items)
3. **AI email drafting** — not a template, a context-aware draft ready to personalise
4. **Live meeting support** — record directly inside the platform, no third-party recorder
5. **Recurring meeting intelligence** — surfaces unresolved action items from previous sessions automatically
6. **Compliance-ready** — PII redaction and audit logging built in, not bolted on

## OBJECTION RESPONSES

**"I already use Otter / Fireflies / Fathom."**
> Those are transcription tools. MeetSummary is a workflow tool — it connects the transcript to your CRM, your email, your team notifications, and your compliance requirements. It's the layer above the transcript.

**"My clients are protective of their data."**
> MeetSummary runs on Supabase with row-level security — each client's data is fully isolated. Compliance Mode adds PII redaction and audit logging for regulated industries.

**"I only handle a few clients."**
> The time saving compounds. Even at 5 clients with 2 meetings each per week, MeetSummary reclaims a full workday per month — and the quality of your deliverables goes up immediately.
